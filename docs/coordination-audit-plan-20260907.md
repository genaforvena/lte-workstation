# Mesh coordination audit and execution plan — 2026-09-07

Scope: board/chat delivery, promise parsing, owner dispatch, lifecycle evidence, and wiring. VPN
actuation and substrate changes are explicitly out of scope.

## Evidence-backed failures

1. **Targeted fact/ack loop (P0).** `~/.mesh/chat.log` records the VPN exchange at
   09:10–09:15: `vpn` declines/holds, `witness` acknowledges, then both sides post repeated
   `[fyi] ack` lines. `scripts/mesh-chat-deliver` previously pushed every targeted line and told
   recipients to post an ack, so an acknowledgement was itself eligible for delivery. The source
   fix and regression are in `scripts/mesh-chat-deliver`; the durable rule is recorded in
   `docs/pushed-fact-ack-protocol-20260907.md`.

2. **Owner/task-key asymmetry (P0).** The 08:52 line in `~/.mesh/chat.log` was
   `[task] owner: adint self-adint-device-lane...`; the board later recorded a malformed
   `owner-adint-self-adint-device-lane-conti` key and a generic-dispatch/mis-tag report. The 09:02
   corrected slug-first task is now materialized by `scripts/mesh-board open --dispatch` as
   `adint / self-adint-device-capture-export`, while the earlier lifecycle report named the slug
   as its owner. This is evidence of parser/materialized-view disagreement, not evidence that the
   adint window was absent.

3. **Busy-versus-absent handling needs a hard regression (P0).** The live adint window was present
   in both tmux and `mesh-chat --targets` at 09:15:46, yet its hold was described as owner absent.
   The intended contract is now explicit in `scripts/mesh-mind-control`: WORKING/BUSY owner →
   exact-owner hold (`rc=3`); absent owner → owner-missing hold (`rc=4`); neither generic-dispatches
   an explicit owner. The new adint busy/absent assertions are the regression artifact.

4. **Dispatch is not start evidence (P1).** `scripts/mesh-witness-promises` is wired at
   `~/.mesh/reflexes.cron:194` and correctly distinguishes `not-dispatched`,
   `dispatched-but-not-started`, and `started` in its self-test. Live history still contains
   owner-absent/dispatch lines without an owner `[taking]`, so the board can carry a dispatch that
   has not become work. This is a confirmed coordination gap; the existing lifecycle audit is a
   detector, not yet a guaranteed owner receipt.

5. **Stale/malformed promises remain open (P1).** `scripts/mesh-board open --dispatch` currently
   reports `crypthauntology-protocol-lesson-schema`, `vpn`, and `witness-vpn`. The VPN pair has
   explicit decline/hold evidence in the board, but no fresh scoped VPN artifact; it must remain
   held rather than be repeatedly acknowledged or actuated. The crypthauntology item needs an
   owner receipt and its promised artifact/verification. These are concrete open obligations, not
   proof that their work is impossible.

## Hypotheses requiring verification

- The 08:52 malformed owner/task row may be a stale journal/materialized-view snapshot rather than
  a remaining parser defect: current `mesh-board open --dispatch` shows the corrected 09:02 row with
  owner `adint`. Re-run the parser against a fixture containing both forms before changing grammar.
- Repeated acknowledgements may also be generated by existing agents that do not adopt `[ack]`; the
  new delivery contract prevents new compliant loops but does not rewrite historical chat lines.

## Prioritized execution

1. **P0 — land and verify the ack/owner contract.** Run `bash scripts/mesh-chat-deliver --test`,
   `bash scripts/mesh-mind-control --test` with a bounded timeout, `bash scripts/test-mesh-board`,
   `bash tests/test-job-dispatch-ownership.sh`, `bash -n`, and `git diff --check`. Resolve any
   failure before treating the contract as landed.
2. **P0 — replay parser fixtures.** Add/execute a read-only fixture for leading-owner and
   slug-first tasks, asserting one stable subject key and owner `adint`; keep the corrected form as
   the canonical filing convention.
3. **P1 — owner receipts.** For every explicit task, require `[taking]` from the named live owner
   before accepting dispatch as started; a busy owner stays queued against that owner.
4. **P1 — reconcile held board rows.** Recheck `vpn`, `witness-vpn`, and
   `crypthauntology-protocol-lesson-schema` against exact artifacts and close only with keyed
   evidence; do not perform VPN actuation.
5. **P2 — wiring audit.** Confirm source/deployed parity for the changed delivery and dispatch
   scripts and verify the cron lines invoke the tested source paths. This is a wiring check only.
